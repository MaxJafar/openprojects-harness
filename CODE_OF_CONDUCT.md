# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

OpenProjects contributors and maintainers are committed to a respectful,
welcoming, and harassment-free community. Participation should be safe for
people of every background, identity, experience level, and ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, specific, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Respect privacy, boundaries, and differing experience levels.
- Accept correction, acknowledge mistakes, and repair harm where possible.
- Keep security and conduct reports confidential while they are reviewed.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, deliberate
intimidation, doxxing, sustained disruption, impersonation, and publishing
someone's private information without permission are not acceptable. Abuse of
project infrastructure, security-reporting channels, or contributor access is
also prohibited.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in repository spaces and whenever someone represents the
project in public or private community interactions. Maintainers may edit or
remove content, reject contributions, restrict participation, or ban a person
when necessary to protect the community.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's
[confidential advisory form](https://github.com/MaxJafar/openprojects-harness/security/advisories/new)
and prefix the title with `Conduct report:`. Do not publish personal or
sensitive incident details in an issue. Reports will be handled as privately
and promptly as practical; conflicts of interest should be disclosed and the
affected maintainer should recuse themselves.

Enforcement decisions may consider context, severity, history, safety, and the
needs of affected people. Retaliation against a reporter or participant in an
investigation is prohibited.
